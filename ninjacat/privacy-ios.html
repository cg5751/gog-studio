요약: Ninja Cat iOS 버전은 게임 진행 정보를 기기 안에 저장합니다. 전 세계 순위표를 위해 한 판이 끝날 때마다 익명 플레이어 ID, 닉네임, 국가, 점수가 Unity Technologies(미국)의 Unity Gaming Services로 전송됩니다. 로그인이나 계정 생성은 없습니다. 광고는 앱을 실행할 때 미리 불러오며, 이때와 보상형 광고를 시청할 때 광고에 필요한 최소한의 정보가 Google 서비스를 통해 처리됩니다. 앱 추적 투명성(ATT) 허용을 요청하지 않으며 광고 식별자(IDFA)로 여러분을 추적하지 않습니다. 인앱결제는 없으며, 개인정보를 판매하지 않습니다.

Summary: The iOS version of Ninja Cat stores game progress on your device. For the worldwide leaderboard, an anonymous player ID, your nickname, your country and your score are sent to Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies, USA) at the end of each run. There is no sign-in and no account to create. Ads are pre-loaded when the app starts; at that time and when you watch a rewarded ad, the minimum information required for ads is processed through Google services. The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track you with the advertising identifier (IDFA). There are no in-app purchases, and we do not sell personal information.

3. 광고

광고는 Google LLC의 Google AdMob을 통해 제공됩니다. 광고 기능을 위해 AdMob SDK는 아래와 같은 정보를 처리할 수 있습니다.

맞춤 광고 제한 방법: iOS 설정 → 개인정보 보호 및 보안 → Apple 광고에서 개인 맞춤 광고를 끌 수 있고, 설정 → 개인정보 보호 및 보안 → 추적에서 앱의 추적 요청 허용을 끌 수 있습니다(본 게임은 요청하지 않습니다). 이 경우에도 광고는 계속 표시되지만 개인 맞춤이 적용되지 않습니다.

1. Information stored on your device

This game stores the following information in your device's internal storage. The developer cannot access it.

  • High score
  • Background music and sound effect volume settings
  • First-launch flag and whether you have finished setting a nickname and country
  • The country code you selected
  • A score that could not be uploaded yet because you were offline (it is sent once you are back online)
  • The anonymous sign-in token for the leaderboard service

Deleting the app deletes this information as well. If iCloud Backup is enabled, some settings may be included in your iCloud backup and restored when you reinstall. That feature is provided by Apple, and the developer cannot see its contents.

2. Worldwide leaderboard (Unity Gaming Services)

The leaderboard is stored in Unity Gaming Services, operated by Unity Technologies, Inc. (USA). The app obtains an anonymous player ID automatically — there is no sign-in and no account to create — and the following information is sent at the end of each run. There is no setting to turn this off; if you do not want it, you can delete your records at any time as described in section 5. The iOS and Android versions share the same leaderboard.

ItemWhat it isPurpose
Anonymous player IDA random string generated by Unity. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or device IDIdentifying your entry on the leaderboard
NicknameThe display name you type in the game (default: "Cat" plus four digits). Visible to other playersDisplaying your entry
CountryA two-letter country code taken from your device's region setting or chosen by you (e.g. KR), shown as a flagDisplaying your entry
Score (height reached)The best height of each run. Only your best record is kept on the serverCalculating and displaying rankings
Connection informationYour IP address and other information needed for the connection may reach Unity's servers as part of the transfer; an approximate (country-level) location may be derived from the IP addressOperating and securing the service

On the leaderboard, your nickname, flag, score and rank are visible to other players. We recommend not putting your real name, contact details or anything else that identifies you in your nickname. You can change your nickname and country at any time on the in-game PROFILE screen. If you see a nickname you consider inappropriate, please let us know at the address in section 8 and we will review it.

Transfer outside your country: this information is transferred to Unity Technologies, Inc. in the United States over the internet at the end of each run and whenever you change your nickname or country, for the purpose of operating the worldwide leaderboard, and is kept until you delete it or the leaderboard service is discontinued. There is no setting to opt out of this transfer; if you do not want it, you can stop using the game or delete your records as described in section 5, in which case the leaderboard cannot be used. Unity's handling of it is governed by the Unity Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising

This game uses rewarded ads only. An ad plays only when you actively choose it in-game — for example, by selecting "watch an ad to continue." If you prefer not to, you can keep playing without watching any ad. We do not use interstitial ads that interrupt play, nor banner ads that stay on screen.

Ads are served through Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC. To deliver ads, the AdMob SDK may process the following information.

ItemPurpose
Device identifiers (app- or developer-scoped identifiers; the advertising identifier IDFA is not used because the app never asks for tracking permission)Serving ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, approximate region estimated from the IP address, etc.)Selecting and delivering appropriate ads
Ad interaction records (impressions, whether the ad was watched to completion, etc.)Granting rewards and measuring ad performance
App performance and diagnostic information (app launch time, hang rate, energy usage, crash logs, etc.)Measuring performance and diagnosing errors in the ad SDK

App Tracking Transparency: this game never requests the iOS "Allow Tracking" permission. Ads are therefore delivered without linking your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

Consent for personalized ads: Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are shown a consent dialog regarding ad personalization when the app starts, and can change that choice at any time in the in-game settings.

Limiting personalized ads: On iOS, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising to turn off personalized ads, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to stop apps from asking to track (this game does not ask). Ads will still be shown, but they will not be personalized.

4. What we do not do

  • No in-app purchases and no handling of payment information
  • No analytics or user-behavior tracking tools of our own — information the ad SDK processes to measure ad performance is described in section 3
  • No access to precise location — this game requests no location permission. Your country comes from your device's region setting or your own choice (section 2), and service providers may estimate an approximate region (country or city level) from your IP address (sections 2 and 3)
  • No access to contacts, photos, call logs, microphone, or camera
  • No tracking with the advertising identifier (IDFA) and no App Tracking Transparency request
  • No sale of personal information to third parties
  • No collection of directly identifying information such as your name, email address, or phone number — your nickname is a display name you choose freely; we never ask for your real name

5. Retention and deletion

Information on your device is deleted when you delete the app.

Leaderboard records and the anonymous account are kept until you delete them. There are two ways:

  • Delete immediately in the game: on the main screen, tap SETTINGS → DELETE MY DATA → DELETE. Your leaderboard records, nickname and country on the server and your anonymous player ID are deleted right away, and the high score and any not-yet-uploaded score on your device are reset. You then start over with a new anonymous ID. This cannot be undone and requires an internet connection.
  • If you have already deleted the app: email gogstudio210327@gmail.com with your nickname, your flag, your approximate best score and when you last played, and we will locate and delete your records. Because only an anonymous ID is stored, we may be unable to act when this information does not single out one entry (for example, when several players share the same nickname).

Retention and deletion of advertising-related information follow Google LLC's policies.
