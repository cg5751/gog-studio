Ninja Cat

by GOG Studio

A vertical arcade climber built around two inputs and one number. A ninja cat leaps between trees, dodging enemy ninjas and traps on the way up. Your score is the height you reach, in meters — nothing else.

How to Play

One input Jump to the tree on the opposite side.
A second, in mid-air Double jump and change direction.
Score The height you climbed, in meters.

That is the entire control scheme. The difficulty comes from what is falling, throwing, and waiting above you.

Features

  • Hand-drawn pixel art, made by one person
  • Portrait, one-handed play on mobile
  • Leaderboards — your own record first, then everyone else's
  • No in-app purchases. The Android version has optional rewarded ads only
